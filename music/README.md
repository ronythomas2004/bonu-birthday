# Music folder

Drop your MP3 file here, named exactly:

  song.mp3

That's it — the site will automatically pick it up and the music toggle button (bottom-right corner) will appear after the lock screen is unlocked.

## Where to get a free MP3

1. **YouTube → MP3**: Use a site like yt1s.com or a browser extension to download the audio of any YouTube video as an MP3. Rename it to song.mp3.
2. **Spotify**: If you have a Spotify track in mind, look it up on YouTube and download from there.
3. **Free music**: pixabay.com/music has hundreds of royalty-free tracks — great for a personal site.

## Recommended vibe
Something soft and emotional — an acoustic version of a song you both love, or a piano instrumental. The music plays on loop at 55% volume.
